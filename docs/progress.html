Batch 11 — Full multi-factor advisory decision engine — COMPLETE

  • Deterministic multi-factor fusion, freshness/coverage/contradiction confidence controls, configurable decision thresholds, and evidence-backed bull/bear/risk/catalyst output.

Batch 12 — Learning and validation layer — COMPLETE

  • Prediction snapshots: immutable historical captures of advisory action, score, confidence, horizon, factor scores and evidence IDs.
  • Outcome tracking: durable SQLite records for realized return, benchmark-relative return and max drawdown.
  • Look-ahead protection: evidence timestamps must not exceed prediction time; outcomes must not be observed inside the declared prediction horizon; ticker mismatches are rejected.
  • Walk-forward validation: chronological train/test windows with optional embargo; calibration training is restricted to outcomes realized before each test boundary.
  • Factor calibration: bounded correlation-based factor multipliers are learned only from prior observations, with conservative caps to reduce overfitting.
  • Validation metrics: directional accuracy, mean return/excess return, drawdown, Brier score, confidence calibration error and action-level results.
  • Regression tests: snapshot provenance, future-evidence rejection, horizon leakage rejection and strictly out-of-sample walk-forward behavior.
